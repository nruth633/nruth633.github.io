Electrical & Embedded Engineering

Nicolas Ruth

I build embedded systems end to end — STM32 firmware in C, the custom PCB it runs on, and the control loop that makes it behave. Currently an engineering student working on RF and hardware design.

What I work with

Hardware, firmware, and the loop between them

Embedded firmware

STM32 in C — F401, F407, and F103 variants. STM32CubeIDE with HAL drivers, peripheral bring-up, and board smoke-testing. Arduino for rapid prototyping.

PCB design

KiCad end to end: schematic capture, layout, custom footprint libraries, BOM and gerber generation. Several boards designed, fabricated, and brought up.

RF & test

Antenna design and S-parameter measurement. LTspice for analog simulation, plus bench work with scopes and signal generators.

Controls & modelling

MATLAB for control system design and analysis — root locus, frequency response, and closed-loop simulation from coursework and lab work.

Mechanical & CAD

Fusion 360 and Autodesk Inventor for enclosures and mounts, with FDM 3D printing to get parts in hand and iterate quickly.

Tooling

Git and GitHub for version control, including keeping design revisions traceable rather than overwritten. Python for scripting and data work.
