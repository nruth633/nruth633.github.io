What I work with

From schematic to soldered board

Power electronics

Boost converters, high-voltage capacitor banks, inrush limiting, and the protection design that keeps a charging stage alive. Hundreds of joules, handled deliberately.

PCB design & fabrication

KiCad schematic capture and layout, including non-standard footprints and drill specs for in-house milling on an LPKF CNC. Designed, fabricated, and soldered.

RF & analog

Antenna design and S-parameter measurement professionally; discrete RF and audio analog design personally, down to hand-wound coils and tuned LC front ends.

Embedded systems

C and embedded C on STM32, Arduino, and Teensy. Sensor integration over I²C and SPI, data logging, and board bring-up.

Simulation & analysis

LTspice for circuit simulation and MATLAB for signals and systems work, checked against real bench measurements with a scope and multimeter.

Mechanical & shop

SolidWorks and Onshape for parts and enclosures, 3D printing, and comfort with hand and power tools — carried over from leading a FIRST Robotics mechanical subteam.

Selected work

Featured projects

  • Electromagnetic Rail Launcher

    A boost converter steps an 11.1 V LiPo up to ~225 V to charge a 13,200 µF bank storing roughly 330 joules, then dumps it across dual aluminium rails to accelerate a projectile — with a protection network so the charging stage survives it.

    • Power electronics
    • High voltage
    • 330 J

  • DemoSat Balloon Payload

    A four-sensor payload PCB designed and soldered for a high-altitude balloon flight — temperature, humidity, pressure, and acceleration over I²C with SD logging, with parts chosen to survive the cold and near-vacuum up there.

    • KiCad
    • Sensors
    • I²C

  • FM Radio Receiver

    A working FM receiver built from discrete parts — a hand-wound 0.15 µH coil and a variable capacitor tuning the whole 88–108 MHz band, an S9018 recovering the signal, and an LM386 driving a speaker.

    • Discrete
