Projects

  • Electromagnetic Rail Launcher

    A 13,200 µF capacitor bank charged to 225 V from an 11.1 V LiPo, about 330 J. Twelve series resistors keep inrush to 85 mA. I picked them for their wattage rather than their resistance. A 600 V diode blocks current from flowing back.

    • Power electronics
    • High voltage

  • Measuring the Silicon Bandgap

    I measured the silicon bandgap and Boltzmann's constant with one transistor, a 9 V battery and a multimeter, then used them to build a flat 1.31 V reference. My numbers were 13–17% off the textbook values, and the page goes through why.

    • Semiconductor physics
    • Measurement

  • DemoSat Balloon Payload

    A redesign of a high-altitude balloon payload after two of its four sensors were discontinued. The new design comes to 120 g and $115, under the 200 g and $147 limits. It was designed but never built.

    • KiCad
    • Sensors
    • Design only
